Signing up for Hikmah Hackers is now online, and your student code comes **straight to your email**. Here's how it works:

1. **Open the sign-up form.** Go to the [Sign up](/signup) page and press **Open the sign-up form**. Fill it in **with a parent or guardian**. It takes about 5 minutes.
2. **Check your email.** In a few minutes, an email from **Hikmah Hackers** arrives with your personal student code. It looks like `KQM-4827`. It goes to your parent's email, and to yours if you gave one. If you don't see it, check spam.
3. **Log in.** Press **Log in** at the top of any page and type your code. You'll see your **My work** page with your progress and teacher feedback.
4. **Turn in your work.** Use the **Submit** button to send your mini-project each week.

**Keep your code private.** It's how you log in and turn in work, so don't share it with other students.

**Already signed up, or lost your code?** Don't fill in the form again. Ask your teacher, or email [faiyajr@umich.edu](mailto:faiyajr@umich.edu), and we'll help.
