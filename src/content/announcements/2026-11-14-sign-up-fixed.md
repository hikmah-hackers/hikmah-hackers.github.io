The sign-up form had a problem: it didn't ask for an email, so student codes weren't being sent. **It's fixed now.**

**Already signed up?** You don't need to sign up again. Come see **Faiyaj** on Friday and ask for your student code. You can also email [faiyajr@umich.edu](mailto:faiyajr@umich.edu).

**Haven't signed up yet?** Go to the [Sign up](/signup) page and fill in the form **with a parent or guardian**. Your student code will come to your email in a few minutes. If you don't see it, check spam.
