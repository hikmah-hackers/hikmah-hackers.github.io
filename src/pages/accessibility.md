## What we've done

- **Works on school Chromebooks:** pages are lightweight and fast, even on slow internet.
- **Keyboard friendly:** everything can be used with a keyboard, with a visible focus outline, and a "Skip to content" link at the top of every page.
- **Readable:** text and buttons are designed to meet strong color-contrast guidelines, and the text resizes with your browser zoom.
- **Screen readers:** pages use clear headings, labelled form fields, and image descriptions, including a text description of every lesson slide.
- **Less motion:** if your device is set to *reduce motion*, the site's animations turn off.
- **Slides your way:** every lesson's slides can be viewed full screen, stepped through with the arrow keys, or downloaded as a PDF.
- **Works without logins:** students use a simple code instead of accounts and passwords.

We aim to follow the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA.

## Known limits

- The **online Python editor** we link to is run by another company, so we can't control how accessible it is. If it doesn't work for you, tell your teacher and we'll find another option.
- The **slide images** are pictures of the slides. The lesson page below them repeats the key ideas and code as real text.

## Tell us if something doesn't work

If anything on this site is hard to use, please tell us. We'll fix it, or help you get the information another way.

Email **faiyajr@umich.edu**, or talk to your teacher.
