## Our promise

*Hikmah* means wisdom. We want every student to feel safe asking questions, making mistakes, and building things they're proud of. These guidelines apply in class, on this website, and anywhere we represent Hikmah Hackers, including competitions.

## Be kind

- Speak to classmates with respect, the way the Prophet ﷺ taught us to treat others.
- Never laugh at someone's question or mistake. Every programmer was a beginner once.
- No bullying, name-calling, or leaving people out.

## Help, don't hand over

- Helping a classmate is great: explain the idea, point to the bug, ask them a question that helps them think.
- Don't type it for them, and don't copy someone else's work and hand it in as your own.
- If you got help or found code online, say so in your note to the teacher. Honesty is part of good character.

## Keep things safe

- Keep your **student code private**, and never use someone else's.
- Don't share personal information (yours or anyone's), like addresses or phone numbers, in your code or notes.
- Only write code meant to learn and build. Never write code meant to trick, harm, or spy on anyone.

## Respect the space

- Look after school Chromebooks and the classroom.
- Follow your school's and the madrasa's rules for devices and the internet.
- When the teacher is talking, it's listening time. Laptop lids half-down helps!

## Keep your work appropriate

Everything we make and submit should be something you'd happily show your parents and teachers: no bad language, inappropriate images, or hurtful jokes.

## If something goes wrong

- If someone makes you uncomfortable, or you see something that breaks these guidelines, tell your teacher or any madrasa staff member. You won't get in trouble for speaking up.
- Breaking these guidelines may lead to a conversation with the teacher, a call home, losing a student code, or leaving the program, depending on what happened.
