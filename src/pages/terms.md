## About these terms

By using this website, the student portal, or the Submit page, you agree to these terms. If you're a student, please read them with a parent or guardian. They're short.

Hikmah Hackers is a free coding program run at Darul Uloom Michigan. "We" means the program's organizers and teachers. "You" means anyone using the site, including students and their families.

## Who can use the class tools

- The **lessons, guide, news, and showcase** are open to everyone.
- The **My work** portal and **Submit** page are for enrolled students only, using the personal student code their teacher gave them.

## Your student code

- Your code is **just for you**. Don't share it, and don't use anyone else's.
- If you think someone else knows your code, tell your teacher and they'll give you a new one.
- We may turn off a code if it's misused, or when a student leaves the program.

## Your work belongs to you

- The code you write and submit is **yours**.
- By submitting, you let us store it, read it, and give feedback, which is the whole point of the class.
- We may feature great projects on the **Showcase** with your first name only. Ask your teacher if you'd rather we didn't, and we'll take it down.

## Using the site the right way

Please don't:

- submit anything hurtful, inappropriate, or that isn't your own work without saying so;
- try to break, overload, or get around the site's security, or access anyone else's work;
- submit code designed to cause harm (for example, code that deletes files or collects other people's information).

Everyone in the class also follows our [Code of Conduct](/code-of-conduct).

## Lesson materials

The lessons, slides, and other materials on this site are made for Hikmah Hackers students. You're welcome to use them to learn and to share them for **non-commercial teaching**. Please credit Hikmah Hackers and don't sell them.

## Other websites

We link to helpful outside tools, such as an online Python editor. We don't control those sites and aren't responsible for their content or how they work. Their own terms and privacy policies apply.

## Disclaimer

We teach coding so students can **build helpful things and solve problems**, and we teach them to use these skills honestly and kindly. Please understand the limits of what we're responsible for:

- **Outside learning.** We're responsible only for what we teach in class and in our own lesson materials. We aren't responsible for anything a student learns from other websites, videos, apps, AI tools, or people, even if they found it while working on a class project.
- **How skills are used.** Students are responsible for how they use what they learn. We aren't responsible for any harmful, illegal, or inappropriate use of coding skills outside class, including on other devices, websites, or services. Using code to harm, trick, spy on, or break into anything is against our [Code of Conduct](/code-of-conduct) and may be against the law.
- **Supervision.** We supervise students only during class time. Outside class, including homework, parents and guardians are responsible for supervising their child's computer and internet use.
- **Outside tools.** The online editors and other sites we link to are run by other organizations under their own terms.

If we learn that a student is misusing what they've learned, we may talk with them and their family, and we may turn off their student code.

## No guarantees

We work hard to keep the site running and correct, but it's provided **as is**. Occasionally things may be unavailable, change, or contain mistakes. Save a copy of your code in your own Google Doc, as we teach in Week 1. To the extent the law allows, we aren't responsible for losses caused by using the site or the class, and nothing in these terms limits any responsibility that can't be limited by law.
